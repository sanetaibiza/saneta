What data we process

The data you give us in the quote form or when you write to us by email or phone: name, phone, email, type of service, frequency, preferred day and time, town and the description of the place. We do not ask for special categories of data.

What we use it for

  • To handle your request and answer your questions.
  • To prepare and send you the quote you ask for.
  • If you accept the quote, to organise and provide the service and manage its invoicing.

We do not use your data to send you advertising and we do not create profiles.

Legal basis

We process your data because you ask us to when you request a quote (pre-contractual measures, article 6.1.b GDPR) and with your consent, which you give by ticking the acceptance box in the form (article 6.1.a GDPR). If you hire the service, the basis is the performance of the contract and compliance with SA NETA's legal obligations.

How long we keep it

If you do not hire the service, we keep your data for a maximum of one year from the last communication. If you do, for as long as the relationship with SA NETA lasts and, afterwards, for the periods required by tax and commercial law.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties, unless required by law. Only to provide their service to us, the technology providers we use may access it: Google, for SA NETA's Gmail account and the tools where we record requests, quotes and services (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Calendar); the website hosting (GitHub); and the service that sends the form to our email (FormSubmit). Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards set out in the GDPR.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability at any time, and withdraw your consent, by writing to sanetaibiza@gmail.com and stating which right you wish to exercise. We will reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access or misuse, and only those who need it to assist you have access to it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the regulations or the way we work change. The version in force is the one published on this page.
