About us

SA NETA

A professional approach to cleaning.

We are from Ibiza and we know how important it is to trust the people who come into your space. That is why we rely on a professional, clearly identified team that is committed to doing things properly.

For us, cleaning is not just about leaving a space spotless. It means looking after every corner, paying attention to the small details and making sure every client feels their space is in good hands.

We work with organisation, responsibility and a personal touch, adapting to the needs of each home, community, office or business.
