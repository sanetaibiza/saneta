Cookie policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can store on your device to remember information about your visit. There are similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage.

What this website uses

This website does not install analytics, advertising or social media cookies, either its own or third-party ones, and the fonts are served from the website itself.

It only stores a technical preference in your browser to remember that you have already accepted the notice shown when you arrive, so that it is not shown again. This preference contains no personal data and is not sent to any server.

Links to external services

The WhatsApp button opens an external service that applies its own privacy and cookie policy.

How to manage or delete this information

You can allow, block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you delete it, the notice will be shown again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If analytics tools, maps, videos or other external services that use cookies are added to the website in the future, we will update this page and ask for your consent where required.
